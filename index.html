The Studio

Small by design.

Zawad Studio is a compact team of designers and engineers working out of Dhaka for clients across Europe, the Gulf, and North America. We take on a limited number of engagements each year — enough to stay solvent, few enough to stay involved in every one of them.

There is no account layer. The people in the first call are the people who draw the type, write the CSS, and answer the email eighteen months after launch. It makes us slower to scale and considerably harder to disappoint.

Ali Zawad — Founder & Creative Director

Process

01 — Discover

Listen before we speak

Stakeholder interviews, audience research, and a full audit of where you stand. We form opinions slowly, on purpose.

02 — Define

Strategy in one page

Positioning, message hierarchy, and success metrics distilled until they fit on a single sheet. If it needs a deck, it isn't sharp enough.

03 — Design

Craft, in the open

Weekly working sessions instead of big reveals. You see the thinking as it happens — and the work is stronger for it.

04 — Build

Engineering as design

Hand-built front ends, accessibility as a baseline, performance budgets enforced from the first commit.

05 — Launch

Ship, then sharpen

Launch is the midpoint, not the finish. We measure, refine, and stay accountable to the numbers we promised.
