§ 11 · Do / Don't

Paired examples.

Left column: banned by name in the guide. Right column: what the system does instead.

Don't · gradient hero
Elevate your business online.
Digital transformation, seamless.

Every agency site does this. Reader sees it and closes the tab.

Do · flat paper hero
You're good. Nobody can find you. We fix that.

Named price, real language, no ornament.

Reads like a supplier, not a marketing agency.

Don't · stock handshake
[Stock handshake photograph]

Every reader has seen this photo elsewhere this week.

Do · one real photograph

Photo — real hand, real tool, real workshop.
Taken on a phone. Never with a filter.

One photo of a real client's work worth more than ten stock images.

Don't · three-card feature grid
🛠
Design
🔧

🚀

Icons + noun triads. Reader has no idea what he'd actually receive.

Do · invoice-formatted line items
Home£99

Reader recognises the shape immediately. It's a receipt.

Don't · "elevate" headline
Elevate your business online

Every word marketing-register. Also a gradient on text. Two fails.

Do · one blunt sentence
Five-page websites for trades.

Names the product, names the buyer.

Don't · rounded card + drop shadow
Card title
Rounded, elevated, floating.
Feels like an app, not a document.

SaaS convention. Reader is not on SaaS.

Do · rectangular block + 1px rule
Block title
Rectangular. Rule border. Sits still.

Reads as a printed document. Ages well.
